# Business Brief: Tekrise Academy Portal

## 1. The Business
**Tekrise Academy** is a practical technology training institute located in Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria. It runs intensive 12-to-16-week cohorts in Product Design, Data Analytics, and Full-Stack Software Engineering through hybrid physical classes at its Ibadan hub and live online sessions. Students pay tuition in structured installments (typically a 60% admission deposit and a 40% mid-cohort balance) and must meet strict attendance and project milestones to graduate.

## 2. The Person Who Uses It
Our primary user is a **19-to-32-year-old aspiring tech professional** living in Ibadan or across South-West Nigeria. They access the portal daily on a mid-range Android smartphone (such as a Redmi, Samsung A-series, or Tecno) and occasionally on a Windows laptop during lab hours. They rely on fluctuating 3G/4G mobile data (MTN or Airtel) or shared hub Wi-Fi. Technically, they are beginners-to-intermediates: comfortable with WhatsApp and mobile banking apps, but easily overwhelmed by cluttered enterprise dashboards.

A secondary user is the **Admissions & Front-Desk Coordinator** at the Ibadan campus, who verifies tuition transfers and issues 5-digit Matriculation PINs (`Matric PINs`) to incoming students.

## 3. The One Thing That Must Work
A newly admitted student who has paid their first tuition installment must be able to claim their pre-assigned 5-digit Matric PIN, create a verified student account, and view their active cohort standing and tuition installment balance without friction.

## 4. Three Questions Only Tekrise Academy Can Answer
1. *"How much is left on my second tuition installment, and what is the exact cut-off date before my portal and physical hub access are paused?"*
2. *"What are the Ibadan hub's physical lab power/generator hours and mentor office-hour schedules for my specific cohort this week?"*
3. *"Have my physical hub attendance check-ins and weekly project grades met the 80% threshold required for my graduation certificate?"*

## 5. Why the Interface Must Feel a Particular Way
Paying tuition in installments and transitioning into a tech career in Nigeria carries financial and emotional weight. The interface must feel **structured, authoritative, and calm—like a modern academic registrar paired with a reliable fintech ledger**. It cannot look like a playful game or a chaotic course marketplace. Clear typographic hierarchy, high-contrast tabular numbers for Naira balances, and generous breathing room reassure students that their tuition records and academic progress are tracked with institutional precision.
