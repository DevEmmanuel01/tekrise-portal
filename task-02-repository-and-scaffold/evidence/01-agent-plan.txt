### 1. Summary of Understanding

#### The Business
* **Organization**: **Tekrise Academy** is an intensive technology training institute based in **Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria**, running 12-to-16-week cohorts in Product Design, Data Analytics, and Full-Stack Software Engineering via hybrid physical classes (at the Ibadan hub) and live online sessions.
* **Target Audience**: 19-to-32-year-old aspiring tech professionals in Ibadan and South-West Nigeria accessing the portal on mid-range Android devices over fluctuating 3G/4G or shared hub Wi-Fi, as well as on Windows laptops during campus lab hours. Admissions & front-desk coordinators represent secondary internal users.
* **Core Value & Tone**: The interface must feel **structured, authoritative, and calm—like a modern academic registrar paired with a reliable fintech ledger**. It addresses three key student questions:
  1. Outstanding second tuition installment balance and exact payment cut-off date before portal and physical hub access are paused.
  2. Ibadan hub physical lab power/generator hours and mentor office hours for the cohort.
  3. Hub attendance check-in and project milestone progress against the mandatory 80% graduation certification threshold.

#### The Stack
* **Framework**: Next.js (App Router, React 19 / 18, TypeScript).
* **Database & ORM**: PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM (`prisma/schema.prisma`).
* **Design & Styling**: Strict consumption of `tokens.css` with CSS custom properties (`--color-*`, `--shadow-*`) and 15 typographic utility classes (`.type-display-lg` through `.type-label-sm`, with `.tabular-nums`).

#### Core Constraints
* **Design Tokens as Single Source of Truth**: `tokens.css` is imported into the global stylesheet. Interface styles must **never** use raw hex codes (`#...`), `rgb()`, `rgba()`, `hsl()`, `hsla()`, primitive `--ref-*` variables, or ad-hoc `font-size`/`font-weight`/`line-height`. Only semantic variables (`--color-*`), typography classes (`.type-*`), and shadow tokens (`--shadow-*`) are permitted.
* **Foundation Only (Zero Scope Creep)**: Only project scaffolding, Prisma setup, and a minimal, responsive home screen at `src/app/page.tsx` introducing Tekrise Academy Ibadan. No authentication, student dashboards, payment flows, or admin consoles.
